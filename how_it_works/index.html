GOOD WORK FINDS A WAY.

Whether you have a task to get done or you’re ready to take one on, Limework helps bring the two sides together.

NEED SOMETHING DONE?

LISTER

Need a hand? List the task and choose the person who feels right for it.

  1. 01

    List your task

    Describe the task, what skills it needs, and the details applicants should know.

  2. 02

    Review applications

    Compare the people who apply and choose who you’d like to do the task.

  3. 03

    Pay Limework after choosing

    Once you select a Task Taker, you pay Limework the amount shown in the app to move the task forward.

  4. 04

    Confirm completion

    When the task is completed, Limework pays the Task Taker. Keep communication clear if plans or scope change.

NEED WORK?

TAKER

Ready to earn? Find a task that suits your skills and apply.

  1. 01

    Explore tasks

    Browse task listings and find work that fits your skills, interests, and availability.

  2. 02

    Apply for the task

    Send your application and share why you’re a good fit. The Task Lister decides who to select.

  3. 03

    Get selected

    If the Task Lister chooses you, they pay Limework through the app before you begin.

  4. 04

    Complete the task and get paid

    Do the agreed work. After completion, Limework pays you according to the details shown in the app.

THE PAYMENT FLOW

Selected by the lister. Paid through Limework. Paid out after completion.

Task Lister selects a Task Taker→Task Lister pays Limework→Task is completed→Limework pays the Task Taker

The Task Lister pays Limework after selecting a Task Taker. When the task is completed, Limework pays the Task Taker. Amounts, payment instructions, completion steps, and payout timing will be shown in the app before you confirm.
