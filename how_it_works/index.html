YOUR JOURNEY

JOB LISTER

Need a hand? Post the job and choose the person who feels right for it.

  1. 01

    List your job

    Describe the work, what skills it needs, and the details applicants should know.

  2. 02

    Review applications

    Compare the people who apply and choose who you’d like to do the job.

  3. 03

    Pay Limework after choosing

    Once you select a worker, you pay Limework the amount shown in the app to move the job forward.

  4. 04

    Confirm completion

    When the job is completed, Limework pays the worker. Keep communication clear if plans or scope change.

YOUR JOURNEY

JOB TAKER

Ready to earn? Find a job that suits your skills and apply.

  1. 01

    Explore jobs

    Browse listings and find work that fits your skills, interests, and availability.

  2. 02

    Apply for the job

    Send your application and share why you’re a good fit. The job lister decides who to select.

  3. 03

    Get selected

    If the lister chooses you, they pay Limework through the app before you begin.

  4. 04

    Complete the job and get paid

    Do the agreed work. After completion, Limework pays you according to the details shown in the app.

THE PAYMENT FLOW

Selected by the lister. Paid through Limework. Paid out after completion.

Job lister selects a worker→Job lister pays Limework→Job is completed→Limework pays the worker

The Job Lister pays Limework after selecting a worker. When the job is completed, Limework pays the worker. Amounts, payment instructions, completion steps, and payout timing will be shown in the app before you confirm.
