A LITTLE MORE ABOUT US 🍋‍🟩

MADE FOR THE WAY WE LIME.

Limework is a Trinidad & Tobago-born platform designed to make it easier to get everyday tasks done, find skilled people, and create more ways to earn.

BUILT WITH TRINIDAD & TOBAGO IN MIND 🇹🇹

OUR WHY

Good work should find its people.

Finding help can be hard. So can finding the next opportunity. Limework brings those needs together in one place, helping people connect around useful work in their communities.

OUR APPROACH

Useful. Local. Welcoming.

We want the experience to feel straightforward for everyone: clear tasks, practical skills, and a simple way to connect. Limework is being shaped for the people and everyday life of Trinidad & Tobago.

MADE TO BE UNDERSTOOD

More languages, more ways to connect.

The Limework app supports Spanish and French translations across the app and listings, helping more people take part. Translation can help people understand a listing, while the people using the service remain responsible for confirming important details with each other.
