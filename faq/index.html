GOT QUESTIONS?

Here’s the simple version of how Limework is planned to work. More details will be shared in the app before launch.

What is Limework?

Limework is a platform planned to connect people who list jobs with people who are looking to take jobs.

How do I get started as a Job Lister?

List the job with the details people need, review the applications, and select the person you want to do it.

How do I get started as a Job Taker?

Browse jobs, apply for work that fits you, and wait for the Job Lister to choose an applicant.

When does payment happen?

After a Job Lister selects a worker, the Job Lister pays Limework through the app. When the job is completed, Limework pays the worker. The app will show the amount, payment steps, and payout timing before people confirm.

Does applying guarantee I’ll get the job?

No. The Job Lister chooses who to select from the applicants.

Can I use Limework in another language?

The app supports Spanish and French translations across the app and job listings. Confirm important details directly, since translations can miss context.

When is Limework launching?

The app launches in 2026, with the desktop website experience planned for early 2027. Follow our socials for launch updates.
