THE QUICK ANSWERS

GOT QUESTIONS?

Quick answers about listing a task, applying, and getting paid through Limework.

What is Limework?

Limework connects people who need tasks done with people ready to use their skills and take on work.

How do I get started as a Task Lister?

List the task with the details people need, review the applications, and select the person you want to do it.

How do I get started as a Task Taker?

Browse tasks, apply for tasks that fit you, and wait for the Task Lister to choose an applicant.

When does payment happen?

After a Task Lister selects a Task Taker, the Task Lister pays Limework through the app. When the task is completed, Limework pays the Task Taker. The app will show the amount, payment steps, and payout timing before people confirm.

Does applying guarantee I’ll get the task?

No. The Task Lister chooses who to select from the applicants.

Can I use Limework in another language?

The app will support Spanish and French translations across the app and task listings. Confirm important details directly, since translations can miss context.

When is Limework launching?

The iOS and Android apps are planned to launch in 2026. The desktop website experience is planned for early 2027. Follow our socials for launch updates.
